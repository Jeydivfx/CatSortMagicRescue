# Rescue sound effects

`sfx_v2/spell_break.wav`: **Glass Break** by **unfa**, CC0 1.0 Universal.
Source: https://freesound.org/people/unfa/sounds/221528/
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Public high-quality preview, shortened to 1.3 seconds, mono, EQ and normalized.
Used as a game sound only, not as generative model training material.

`sfx_v2/escape.wav`: original procedural soft paw patter and air sweep, generated
by `scripts/prepare_escape_audio.py`. No third-party recording used.

Glass plays once at the first visible jar break; escape plays once as kittens
launch. Both follow effects volume/mute and focus/ad suspension.
