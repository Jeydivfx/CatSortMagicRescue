# Audio credits

Current victory audio: win_fanfare.wav and star_0.wav through star_2.wav are original locally composed/synthesized sounds generated by scripts/compose_victory.py. They replace the original win.wav for the completion sequence.

kitten_source.wav: **Kitten Mew**, AntumDeluge (2018), CC0 1.0.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/kitten-mew
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The author describes it as a performed imitation of a kitten. kitten_0.wav through kitten_3.wav are trimmed, normalized, pitch-shifted variations made for this game.

cozy_theme.wav: original 40-second instrumental composition and procedural synthesis for Cat Sort Lab, 96 BPM. Bell melody, plucked arpeggios, bass and a small circular delay; rendered by scripts/compose_audio.py. No external music samples.

tap.wav, land.wav, nope.wav and win.wav: original synthesized game effects. Older meow.wav and ambient.wav are retained as prototype sources and are no longer played as the meow or background track.
