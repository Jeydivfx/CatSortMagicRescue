# Cat scale calibration and witch obstacle

Nine active breeds use the same target occupied silhouette area (4700 logical square pixels at neutral scale), with heights constrained to 75–84 pixels and width at most 82. This preserves each breed's aspect ratio and anchors every paw baseline at y=40. White Persian was approximately 23% fuller than the original tabbies at equal height; Ragdoll was also larger. Calibration now applies before construction of the animated mesh, so idle, drag and victory share the same proportions. Source PNG pixels and alpha are unchanged. assets/cats/size_profiles.json records measured opaque areas; scripts/measure_cat_sizes.gd exposes runtime frame geometry.

The witch's temporary seal starts only after 4–6 successful moves on eligible rounds (40% of level indices, first eligible stage 17). At most one seal per round, 2.6 seconds. A candidate must be incomplete and leave another legal move available. No curse in beginner stages, with one group left, during entry, active drag, victory or loss. The staff impact activates it; purple outline, lock and countdown ring identify the affected tube. Other tubes work normally. Blocked input costs no move, undo or coins. The timer pauses with menus/ads/focus suspension, expires automatically, and clears on a new level, undo, hint, victory or defeat. Contents and solution certificates never change.

Tests: verify_sizes_curse.gd checks all silhouettes, mesh/paw geometry, real impact activation, blocked input, alternate moves, pause/focus suspension, expiry and repeat limits. Existing verify_pacing_magic.gd and verify_cinematic.gd still pass. Screenshots: cat_size_comparison.png and witch_curse_preview.png.
