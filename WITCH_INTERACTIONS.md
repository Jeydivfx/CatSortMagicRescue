# Witch interactions and move budget

The witch remains a visual antagonist. Her actions never alter tube contents,
block a legal move or change the puzzle solution. Nine active kitten designs
are retained.

- Sorted tube: 0.9-second frown and staff strike, timed ground sound and purple
  particles. The kittens cheer; the top kitten leans and grins back.
- Good move: transferring at least two matching kittens without completing a
  tube causes surprise, enlarged eyes, a small recoil and an exclamation mark.
- Idle: after at least eight seconds without a successful move, she may laugh
  with her voice. It is spaced 16–25 seconds apart; never during dragging,
  ads, pauses, near-victory panic, loss or the rescue sequence.
- Invalid destination: brief pleased wand wave with purple particles; no move
  cost. A three-second reaction cooldown prevents repeated teasing.
- One group remaining: eyes look toward the board, a sweat drop and trembling
  staff appear. The purple protective arc is decorative only.
- Rescue: flushed face, hops, ear steam and a temporarily dropped staff;
  existing glass, escape, upset and impact sounds remain synchronized.
- Loss: the witch dances, laughs and circles purple magic while the recovery
  panel offers retry, undo (including the existing rewarded route) or Home.

Reactions are non-blocking. Short reactions last approximately 0.8–1 seconds;
only rescue and loss have dedicated end-of-round presentation. Gentle motion
reduces amplitude while preserving readable expressions and opaque escapes.

For a three-star target T, the two-star threshold is ceil(7T/6), with at least
one extra move; the hard limit is ceil(4T/3), with at least two extra moves.
The two-star threshold always stays below the hard limit. Example: 12/14/16.
Each valid grouped transfer costs one move, whether dragged or tapped.
Invalid drags, selection, hints and animations do not consume moves.
Solving on the final allowed move wins. Reaching the limit unsolved loses and
awards no coins or score. Undo after loss restores the previous board and one
move; retry resets the level and three free undos. Ads cannot grant duplicate
recovery rewards. Targets remain tied to the audited grouped goals for the
first 100 levels and solution certificates for later levels.
