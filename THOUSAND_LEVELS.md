# 1,000-level campaign and grouped dragging

All 1,000 levels have solution certificates replayed in Python and in Godot. The original first 100 layouts/solutions remain intact. New candidates are rejected using a color/tube-invariant graph signature. The estimated mixing score orders later levels within cat-count cohorts; this proves solvability, not optimal solutions or human difficulty balance.

Four new illustrated breeds are introduced at levels 126 (Tuxedo), 251 (Snowball), 501 (Siamese), and 751 (Forest Tabby). Later boards contain up to 11 cat types. Chapters have 10 levels each, with a dropdown to reach any of the 100 chapters. Large boards use three rows; controls remain above the banner reservation.

The new art is assets/cats_advanced.png, generated with built-in ImageGen using the existing expression atlas as a style reference. Final prompt: transparent 4x4 atlas with black-and-white tuxedo, white fluffy blue-eyed, cream/chocolate Siamese, brown/green-eyed tabby cats in columns, and neutral/blink/happy/grumpy expressions in rows. Preserve full seated bodies, paws, tails, proportions and identity across expressions; no labels, props or background.

Drag a tube's top contiguous same-type group to an empty tube or matching top cat. Transfer is limited by destination capacity. Mixed types never move together; invalid drops/cancellation do not change the puzzle. One drag is one move and one Undo snapshot, including a group transfer. Taps retain single-cat transfers, so stored hints/solutions still apply.

The victory cat now has a subdivided 2D mesh rig: independently moving paw, head and tail, recurring soft hops, body sway and happy facial animation. Gentle motion reduces these movements. The overlay's audio and controls retain their previous behavior.
